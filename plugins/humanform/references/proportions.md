# Proportions: where the numbers come from and how they are measured

All values are fractions of stature **H**: floor (lowest vertex) to the top of the body mesh.
Hair and eyes are separate objects and are not counted.

## Sources

| preset | basis | confidence |
|---|---|---|
| `realistic` | **ANSUR II** (US Army anthropometric survey, 2012; public data from Penn State OpenLab, 2017): 4,082 men and 1,986 women aged 17-58, 93 direct measurements. Per sex: mean ratio to stature; warn beyond 1.5 sd, fail beyond 3 sd. Generated by `scripts/derive_anthropometry.py` into `data/presets.json` and `data/anthropometry.json` | measured. A military sample: fitter and younger than the general population (female BMI mean 25.5, male 27.7) |
| `stylized` | Loomis heroic 8-head canon: crotch at mid-height, long legs, shoulders about 2 heads wide on a man | artistic canon, hand-written, checked for self-consistency by the Phase 2 test |
| `chin`, `heads` (both) | Drillis & Contini (1966) via Winter, *Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement*: chin 0.870 H | ANSUR II has no menton height |

### Skin landmarks to joint centres

ANSUR measures points on the skin; a rig and humancheck's joint heights are joint centres. The
offsets, applied as fractions of H (stated once, in `derive_anthropometry.py`):

| joint | from | offset | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| shoulder | acromial height | -0.024 H | the glenohumeral centre sits ~4 cm below the acromion |
| hip | trochanterion height | -0.003 H | the femoral head centre is about level with the greater trochanter's tip |
| knee | lateral femoral epicondyle height | 0 | the flexion axis runs through the epicondyles |
| ankle | lateral malleolus height | +0.003 H | the talocrural axis is just above the malleolus's centre |
| upper arm | acromion-radiale length | -0.017 H | the humerus starts at the GH centre, ~3 cm down the arm from the acromion |

Hip-joint spacing (0.30 x hip breadth) and all depths (y) in `landmarks.py` are approximations;
the base mesh is authoritative for them.

### What ANSUR II settled

- **Upper arm.** Acromion-radiale is 0.191 H for both sexes, matching Drillis & Contini's 0.186
  - so the joint-to-joint upper arm is about 0.174 H. MPFB2's `game_engine` rig gives 0.145 H:
  its arm is ~5 cm short, as are its forearm, hand and (female) foot. Phase 3 lengthens them with
  MPFB's own `measure-*-length` targets.
- **Crotch** sits at 0.480 H (female) and 0.482 H (male), not the canon's 0.47.
- **Widths** replaced estimates: bideltoid 0.277 / 0.291 H, hip breadth 0.217 / 0.197 H (female /
  male); shoulder-to-hip 1.28 / 1.48; waist-to-hip circumference 0.84 / 0.92.

## Definitions

| measure | how |
|---|---|
| joint heights | rig bone heads in rest pose (`upper_arm`/`upperarm`/`LeftArm` for the shoulder, and so on), averaged left and right; MPFB `joint-*` vertex groups when there is no rig |
| `chin` | midline front profile (horizontal slices 4 mm apart, points within 2 cm of x = 0): down from the nose tip until the profile falls back 70% of the way to the throat |
| `heads` | H / (top - chin) |
| `crotch` | scanning up from the knees, the first height at which one cross-section crosses the midline, refined by bisection |
| `upper_arm`, `forearm`, `thigh`, `shin` | distances between joint landmarks |
| `hand` | wrist to the farthest skin along the forearm's direction, within 6% of H of that axis |
| `foot` | extent along the facing direction of the skin below the ankle on that side |
| `shoulder_width` | width of the midline-crossing section at shoulder-joint height - over the deltoids, as ANSUR's bideltoid breadth, not the hanging arms |
| `hip_width`, `hip_circ` | widest midline-crossing section between the crotch and 5% of H above the hip joints (ANSUR hip breadth, buttock circumference) |
| `waist_circ` | section at ANSUR's navel (omphalion) height for the sex, 0.60 H; `waist_min_circ` is the narrowest section between 0.58 and 0.68 H, which on most women is several cm smaller |
| `chest_circ` | section at ANSUR's chest height for the sex |
| circumferences | convex hull perimeter of the section - what a tape measure reads |
| `fingers` | sections across the hand at 62%, 72% and 82% of its length, counting loops; the most found |
| `face_relief` | nose tip in front of the deepest midline point 1.2-5 cm above it (the nasion) |
| `knee/elbow_centering` | section perpendicular to the limb through the joint; distance from joint to section centre, in section radii |
| `symmetry_p95` | 95th percentile distance from mirrored vertices to the surface |
